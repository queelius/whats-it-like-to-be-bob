% About the Author
\chapter*{About the Author}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Author}

Alex Towell writes hard science fiction and philosophical horror. He holds two master's degrees (Computer Science and Mathematics) from Southern Illinois University Edwardsville, where he is completing a PhD in Computer Science with research in information retrieval and Bayesian statistics.

His fiction takes the physics seriously. His characters wish it wouldn't.

He lives in Illinois.
