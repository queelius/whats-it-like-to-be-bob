# Craft Auditor Report

**Date**: 2026-03-26
**Scope**: Full lore bible (12 documents), "What's It Like To Be Bob?"
**Focus**: Prose quality of lore documents, clarity, expository effectiveness, mechanical patterns

---

## Prefatory Note

These are lore documents, not manuscript prose. They are writing about writing -- planning documents, not final text. The craft evaluation here focuses on whether the lore docs are clear enough to serve their function (guiding prose writing), whether the conceptual exposition is effective, and whether the writing in the docs reveals any tendencies that might carry into the manuscript as weaknesses.

---

## Findings

### FINDING K-1: The Bob Document Is Already Manuscript-Quality Prose
- **Severity**: N/A (Strength)
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Location**: bob.md, especially "The Inner Life the ASI Cannot Access" and "The Tuesday" sections
- **Finding**: bob.md is not a planning document. It is prose. The section on Bob's inner life -- the shower thought, the gap between his love for Karen and his ability to express it, the fear about Maya, the four seconds on the bridge -- is finished literary writing that could appear in the manuscript nearly verbatim. The specificity is extraordinary (shoe size 10.5, buys 11s; the box cutter scar; the asymmetric smile). The Tuesday timeline with its data-source column is both a worldbuilding table and a narrative device. This document demonstrates that the author can execute the Bob chapter.
- **Suggestion**: When writing Chapter VI, consider using bob.md's inner-life passages almost directly. They embody the style guide's principles (precision, tenderness, show-don't-lecture) better than the style guide's own examples do.

### FINDING K-2: Repetitive Conceptual Pattern -- The "This Is Not X / It Is Y" Construction
- **Severity**: MEDIUM
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Location**: Multiple documents
- **Finding**: The lore bible relies heavily on a rhetorical pattern: define something by negation, then offer a qualified positive. This is effective in moderation but becomes a verbal tic across the corpus. Examples:
  - world.md: "This is not death. It is not rebirth. It has no human analogue."
  - world.md: "The galaxy is not an empire. It is an ecosystem"
  - interiority.md: "What happened next was not a decision, though it functioned as one. It was not curiosity... It was not love..."
  - themes.md: "This is not tragic. It is the condition that makes everything meaningful."
  - style.md: "Not stream-of-consciousness... Not internal-monologue... Not omniscient-narrator... Something else."
  - probe.md: "The probe is not human. It is not a 'ship' carrying passengers."
  - world.md: "Not 'uploaded' to a computer. The matter BECAME computation."
  - stellar-system.md: "Not a person. Not a brain writ large. Not a god."
- **Impact**: In a lore bible, this is functional -- it efficiently eliminates wrong mental models. But if this pattern carries into the manuscript at the same frequency, it will become conspicuous. The negation-then-qualification structure distances the reader from direct experience. In the Bob chapter especially, where immediacy matters, this pattern would be a liability.
- **Suggestion**: Inventory this pattern before manuscript writing. Reserve it for moments where the negation is genuinely surprising or load-bearing. In the manuscript, prefer direct positive description when possible.

### FINDING K-3: Overuse of Parenthetical Asides
- **Severity**: LOW
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Location**: Multiple documents, especially world.md, physics.md
- **Finding**: The documents use parenthetical asides frequently, sometimes nested. Examples:
  - world.md: "Whether they were 'absorbed,' 'transcended,' 'destroyed,' or 'transformed' depends on definitions that no longer have stable referents."
  - world.md: "each Planck time ($\sim 5.4 \times 10^{-44}$ s), the intelligence of the reorganized matter is measurably greater than the Planck time before"
  - physics.md: "(not because it's trapped, but because it's emitted so slowly that for practical purposes it never arrives)"
  - probe.md: "(if it has one)"
  - interiority.md: "(if that word applies)"
  - interiority.md: "(if experience applies)"
- **Impact**: In lore docs, parentheticals are efficient. In manuscript prose, they break rhythm and can become a crutch. The "(if X applies)" construction is particularly frequent and reflects the book's genuine philosophical uncertainty, but it risks becoming mannered.
- **Suggestion**: In the manuscript, find alternatives to the "(if X applies)" parenthetical. The uncertainty can be conveyed through sentence structure, verb choice, or strategic silence rather than constant hedging asides.

### FINDING K-4: The Style Guide's Example Prose Contains a Date Error and May Be Misleading
- **Severity**: MEDIUM
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Location**: style.md, "The Sublime Through Precision" section
- **Finding**: The "example of RIGHT" in the style guide contains a factual error: "March 14, 2024" should be "March 14, 2028." Beyond the error (flagged by the consistency auditor), the example itself -- while illustrating the precision-over-vagueness principle -- is extremely long (a single sentence running ~60 words). If this is the model for manuscript prose, the density may be unsustainable over chapter-length sections. The example is brilliant as a single sentence; it would be exhausting as a paragraph.
- **Suggestion**: Fix the date. Consider adding a note that this density is for peak moments, not sustained default. The best manuscript prose will vary between this density and more spacious rhythms.

### FINDING K-5: The Archaeology Document Achieves an Exceptional Balance
- **Severity**: N/A (Strength)
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Location**: archaeology.md
- **Finding**: archaeology.md is the lore bible's most accomplished piece of conceptual writing. The five schools are developed with rigor, each with clear methodology, assumptions, blind spots, and philosophical ancestry. The running example (how each school treats the 8:03 AM moment on the bridge) is a masterpiece of parallel structure that simultaneously explains the schools AND dramatizes the book's core theme. The final paragraph -- "None of them are wrong. None of them capture what the READER knows from bob.md" -- is the single best articulation of the book's deepest structural move. This document demonstrates that the conceptual framework is not just intellectually sound but narratively powerful.

### FINDING K-6: Interiority.md's Five Prose Strategies Are Uneven in Development
- **Severity**: LOW
- **Confidence**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: interiority.md, "How to Render This in Prose" section
- **Finding**: Strategies 1 (Architecture) and 2 (Negative Rendering) have fully developed example passages that demonstrate the technique. Strategies 3 (Reader Projection), 4 (Temporal Gradient), and 5 (Bob as Mirror) are described conceptually but Strategy 4 is the only one with an example. Strategy 3 (arguably the most important, since it is the book's central structural move) has no example passage.
- **Suggestion**: Develop example passages for Strategy 3 and 5 before manuscript writing. These strategies are the hardest to execute and would benefit from concrete prototyping.

### FINDING K-7: The Physics Document's Expository Tone Shifts Between Sections
- **Severity**: LOW
- **Confidence**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: physics.md
- **Finding**: Sections I-IV are written in a clean, textbook-adjacent register: clear definitions, tables, precise numbers. Section V (the Unification Framework) shifts to a more speculative, discursive register with longer paragraphs, narrative justification, and exploratory asides. Section VI (Relativistic Wavefront Dynamics) returns to the clean register with worked examples. Section VII (Derived Constraints) is a concise list. This tonal variation is natural for a document that spans canonical physics and speculative worldbuilding, but the shift at Section V is abrupt.
- **Impact**: Not a problem for the lore bible itself, but the author should be aware that the speculative physics (Section V) needs a different prose treatment in the manuscript than the established physics (Sections I-IV). The style guide doesn't address this distinction.
- **Suggestion**: Add a note to style.md about how to handle speculative vs. established physics in the manuscript. The speculative physics should feel like physics being discovered, not physics being stated.

### FINDING K-8: Probe Document's Emotional Power May Be Underestimated
- **Severity**: N/A (Strength, with caution)
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Location**: probe.md
- **Finding**: probe.md achieves something remarkable: it makes a non-human, non-anthropomorphized computational entity emotionally compelling without violating the style guide's prohibition against anthropomorphism. The "unclassified 15%" is a brilliant narrative device -- it creates the space for the reader to project emotion without the text assigning any. The parallel structure table (Bob vs. 7447-Lyra) is elegant and structurally rigorous. The sentence "The probe crossed the dark between stars and it MEANT something, even if what it meant is unknowable" is the kind of line that could close a chapter.

---

## Overall Assessment

The lore bible is not just functional planning material -- several documents (bob.md, archaeology.md, probe.md, interiority.md) are themselves accomplished pieces of expository writing that demonstrate the author's ability to execute the project. The conceptual clarity is exceptional: every document knows exactly what it is doing and why. The primary craft concerns are mechanical patterns (negation-then-qualification, parenthetical hedging) that are appropriate in planning documents but should be monitored in manuscript prose.
