# Voice Auditor Report

**Date**: 2026-03-26
**Scope**: Full lore bible (12 documents), "What's It Like To Be Bob?"
**Focus**: Analytical voice consistency, register shifts, conceptual vocabulary, voice planning for manuscript

---

## Prefatory Note

Since there is no manuscript and no traditional characters with dialogue, this report evaluates: (a) the consistency of the analytical voice across the lore bible, (b) the planned voice differentiation for manuscript chapters, and (c) the treatment of non-human "characters" (the Sol-mind, the probe, the schools of interpretation) in the lore docs.

---

## Findings

### FINDING V-1: The Lore Bible Has a Consistent, Distinctive Authorial Voice
- **Severity**: N/A (Strength)
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Location**: All 12 documents
- **Finding**: Despite covering topics from quantum mechanics to a man's coffee preference, the lore bible maintains a recognizable voice throughout. This voice is characterized by:
  - **Directness**: Short, declarative sentences when stating facts. "The hard problem persists." / "$c$ is an absolute limit. No exceptions."
  - **Controlled escalation**: Building from technical precision to philosophical implication within a single paragraph. The typical arc is: state the physics, derive the consequence, then land the emotional or philosophical punch.
  - **Strategic informality**: The author drops into conversational register at precisely calibrated moments. "Not because it sounds impressive" / "is garbage" / "is a capital offense" / "the way a reader opens a book to a random page."
  - **Refusal to simplify**: The voice never talks down. It assumes the reader can handle Bekenstein bounds and Nagel references in the same sentence. This is the voice of someone who finds no tension between precision and feeling.
- **Impact**: This voice IS the book's voice, at least for the lore-document level. The manuscript will need to differentiate voices per chapter (as the style guide and outline propose), but the underlying sensibility -- precision-as-tenderness, rigor-as-wonder -- should persist.

### FINDING V-2: Planned Voice Differentiation Lacks Concrete Examples
- **Severity**: MEDIUM
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Location**: style.md ("Narrative Voice Guidelines"), outline.md (per-chapter voice descriptions)
- **Finding**: The style guide describes per-chapter voices in adjective clusters:
  - Cosmological: "Observational... Precise, measured, occasionally struck by scale."
  - Wavefront: "Kinetic... Rapid, accumulating, accelerating."
  - Stellar system: "Contemplative... Long sentences. Nested clauses."
  - Bob: "Warm... Detailed, specific, grounded in sensory particulars."
  - Consciousness: "Philosophical. Spare... Every sentence load-bearing."
  - Deep time: "Elegiac. Slow... Verbs shifting from active to passive."
  These are evocative descriptions but they remain abstract. Only the Bob chapter has a concrete reference point (bob.md). The other chapter voices exist as intentions, not demonstrations.
- **Suggestion**: Before writing the manuscript, produce a 500-word prototype passage for each chapter voice. The interiority.md examples (Strategies 1, 2, 4) are a start for the stellar-system and consciousness voices. The cosmological, wavefront, and deep-time voices have no prototype text in the lore bible.

### FINDING V-3: The Sol-Mind's "Voice" Is Well-Established by Constraint
- **Severity**: N/A (Strength)
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Location**: interiority.md, style.md (anti-patterns)
- **Finding**: The lore bible takes an unusually rigorous approach to the Sol-mind's voice: rather than defining what the Sol-mind sounds like, it defines what the Sol-mind CANNOT sound like. The constraints are:
  - No human emotions attributed directly ("felt curious" = forbidden)
  - No human motivation ("decided to study Bob because" = forbidden)
  - No monolithic self ("it decided" implies unity that may not exist)
  - Functional states described externally ("allocated 0.3% of processing" = permitted)
  - Negative rendering permitted ("not a decision, though it functioned as one")
  These constraints, combined with the five prose strategies in interiority.md, create a voice-by-subtraction that is more precise than a positive voice description would be. The author knows exactly what the Sol-mind doesn't sound like, which is more useful than knowing what it does sound like.

### FINDING V-4: Bob's Voice Is Established But the ASI-Studying-Bob Voice Is Not
- **Severity**: MEDIUM
- **Confidence**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: bob.md, archaeology.md, outline.md
- **Finding**: bob.md brilliantly establishes Bob as a specific person. But the Bob chapter (VI) is not narrated by Bob -- it is the ASI's reconstruction of Bob, presented to the reader. This means the Bob chapter has TWO voices operating simultaneously: (a) Bob's life as rendered in warm, specific, literary-fiction prose, and (b) the ASI's analytical frame overlaying that life. The lore bible does not demonstrate how these two voices coexist in a single passage. archaeology.md shows the schools analyzing Bob (analytical voice). bob.md shows Bob's life directly (warm voice). But the manuscript chapter needs to do BOTH AT ONCE.
- **Suggestion**: Develop a prototype passage that interleaves the ASI's reconstruction frame with Bob's lived experience. The Tuesday timeline in bob.md (with its data-source column) gestures toward this, but in prose form the challenge is harder. How does a single passage convey both "Bob noticed the light on the river" and "this moment is reconstructed from vehicle telemetry and weather data; the subject's phenomenal experience is Class X"?

### FINDING V-5: The Five Schools Have Subtly Distinct Analytical Registers
- **Severity**: N/A (Strength)
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Location**: archaeology.md
- **Finding**: The five schools are not just conceptually distinct -- they have distinct prose voices in their analysis of the 8:03 AM moment:
  - **Positivist**: Clipped, bureaucratic. "Subject's vehicle crossed bridge. No observable anomaly in telemetry."
  - **Modal**: Probabilistic, conditional. "Counterfactual weather models show behavior divergence correlated with visual conditions..."
  - **Constructionist**: Technical, model-centric. "Simulated Bob, calibrated to known behavior, shows positive-affect activation..."
  - **Structuralist**: Contextual, systemic. "The subject's commute route crossed the Scioto River due to the spatial distribution of residential and commercial zones..."
  - **Apophatic**: Careful, absence-focused. "Something happened here that we cannot access."
  These voice distinctions are consistent and well-differentiated. If the manuscript includes passages from the schools' perspectives, these registers can be used directly.

### FINDING V-6: The Probe's "Voice" Presents a Unique Challenge
- **Severity**: LOW
- **Confidence**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: probe.md, outline.md (Chapter VIII)
- **Finding**: The outline describes the Probe chapter's tone as "Disorientation. Vertigo. The experience of time-as-weapon." probe.md establishes the probe as "roughly planet-scale in intelligence... compressed into a mass of ~10^9 kg." The probe is not human, but it is from an earlier, more comprehensible era than the stellar-scale minds. The probe's voice falls in a gap: it should be alien (not human) but comprehensible (not as alien as the Sol-mind). The lore bible does not establish how the probe "speaks" or how its perspective is rendered.
- **Suggestion**: The probe's 364-year journey is the longest stretch of sustained perspective in the book. Develop a voice prototype for the probe before writing Chapter VIII. Key decision: is the probe chapter narrated from the probe's perspective (challenging, since the probe's cognition is beyond human) or from the outside (the Sol-mind analyzing the probe's journey, as a parallel to the ASI analyzing Bob)?

### FINDING V-7: Consistent Use of Emphatic Caps for Conceptual Stress
- **Severity**: LOW
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Location**: Multiple documents
- **Finding**: The lore bible uses ALL-CAPS for conceptual emphasis at key moments: "The matter BECAME computation" / "what it was LIKE" / "EVERY human receives this treatment" / "The matter that was Bob is thinking about Bob" / "it MEANT something." This is effective in lore documents for signaling importance. In manuscript prose, it would be distracting.
- **Suggestion**: In the manuscript, replace caps emphasis with prose emphasis -- sentence position, rhythm, or isolation for weight. The moment the caps are highlighting can usually be served by making it a standalone sentence or placing it at the end of a paragraph.

---

## Overall Assessment

The lore bible demonstrates a strong, consistent authorial voice that is well-suited to the project's ambitions. The primary voice challenge for the manuscript is not finding a voice but differentiating multiple voices while maintaining the underlying sensibility. The Bob chapter's dual-voice requirement (warm literary fiction + ASI analytical frame) is the single hardest voice challenge in the book and deserves prototype work before full drafting.
