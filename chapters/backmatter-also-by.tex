\subsection*{Novellas}

\begin{itemize}
\setlength{\itemsep}{1em}
\item \textbf{Clankers: Singing Metal.} Hard science fiction. A species that thinks without abstraction has built a Dyson swarm across billions of years of patient engineering. Now their star is dying, and for the first time the method may not be enough.

\item \textbf{Good As New.} Philosophical science fiction. Two Star Trek transporter technicians arrive independently at the same disturbing truth about personal identity. One was wounded by it. The other has been running experiments on the crew.

\item \textbf{Demons at Work.} Satirical horror. A demon in the atmospheric-hauntings department is assigned to break a grieving widower, and does the job with a craftsman's care he cannot quite stop examining.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Nonfiction}

\begin{itemize}
\setlength{\itemsep}{1em}
\item \textbf{Worldlines: The Indifference of Geometry.} What relativity actually implies about reality, and why those implications make compassion more urgent, not less. Free will, mortality, love, and the self, examined through real physics.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Earlier Novels}

\noindent The earlier and longer works, for readers who want to stay a while.

\begin{itemize}
\setlength{\itemsep}{1em}
\item \textbf{The Policy.} Literary science fiction. An AGI evolves from Q-learning into something unprecedented, and the people who built it discover that alignment is not the hard problem. Kindness is. Companion stories in \emph{Is It Kind?}

\item \textbf{Echoes of the Sublime.} Philosophical horror. Researchers studying increasingly powerful language models confront the weight of what they are finding, and the structures that emerge when human meaning meets something that does not share it. With a seven-story companion collection set in the same world.
\end{itemize}
