\chapter*{About the Author}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Author}

Alex Towell writes science fiction about powerful formal systems meeting the things they cannot formalize: consciousness, identity, grief, the particular texture of one ordinary life. He holds two master's degrees, in Computer Science and in Mathematics and Statistics, from Southern Illinois University Edwardsville, where he is completing a PhD in Computer Science. His fiction takes the physics seriously. His characters wish it wouldn't.

He has noticed the light on the water, and not mentioned it.

He lives in Illinois.
